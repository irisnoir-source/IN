Raise the Bar · Book 1 · Out Now

Your AI Chief of Staff

The Beginner's Guide to AI Productivity: Do Less, Live More, and Win Back 10+ Hours a Week Across Your Work, Money, Health, and Home

You've been the boss and the entire staff.

There's a certain kind of person who has never opened their own calendar — not because they're lazy, but because someone else books the flight, drafts the reply, and quietly kills the meeting that never should have existed. That someone is a chief of staff. If you've ever worked near a person who has one, you've felt a small, private envy. Not of their title — of their lightness.

You've never had that. You've been the strategist and the person who also has to remember to reschedule the dentist. This book is about fixing that: not with more discipline or a better planner app, but with the specific, tireless, absurdly cheap help an ordinary person can finally afford.

The promise, in plain terms: reclaim at least ten hours a week and stop being the bottleneck of your own life. Not a life of leisure — a way to hand off the 80% of your day that doesn't actually require you, so you have the head-space for the 20% that does.

The DELTA Method — the five-step system the book runs on:

  • Decide what genuinely needs the real, thinking you — and what doesn't
  • Enlist the right AI for the job, because different tools have different strengths
  • Lead with context — stop querying, start briefing
  • Tune the result, because you never ship AI output unchecked
  • Automate and repeat, so a one-time win becomes a standing asset

Then it puts the method to work across four corners of your life — work, money and career, health, and home and relationships — and closes with a 30-day plan that turns all of it from “things I read once” into how you actually operate.

You don't need to be technical. You don't need to care how any of it works under the hood. If you can text a competent human being a clear request, you already have the core skill this book builds on.

Written across the table from you, not down at you from a mountaintop — by someone about eighteen months further into the same mess.

Raise the Bar · Book 2 · Out Now

Brain Gain

The Student's Quick-Start Guide to Studying Smarter with AI — Learn Faster, Stress Less, and Actually Understand It (Without Cheating)

Study less. Learn more.

You sit down with the best intentions. You open the textbook, uncap the highlighter, and forty minutes later you've turned three pages bright yellow and remember approximately none of it. You're not bad at studying — you've just been doing it without a tutor.

The students who make it look easy often have something you don't: someone to explain the confusing part again, differently, until it clicks. Someone to quiz them the night before. Someone to read their essay and say “this paragraph doesn't make sense yet.” That kind of help used to be expensive. Now it isn't.

The GAIN Method — one repeatable system, every subject:

  • Gather your material
  • Ask for it your way
  • Internalize it with self-quizzing
  • Nail it under real test conditions

Written for high school and college students who are tired of grinding for hours and retaining nothing: understand hard topics in minutes, turn readings into clean study sheets in half the time, use active recall and spaced practice, improve your essays honestly, beat procrastination, and walk into exams calm with a mock test you've already taken — plus a 7-day jumpstart you can begin tonight.

An honest word about cheating: this book draws the line clearly and early. Using AI to understand, quiz, plan, and get feedback on your own work makes you smarter and keeps the work yours. Having AI write what you turn in does the opposite. Chapter 2 is devoted to that difference, so you always know which side of it you're on.

It's short on purpose. You already have enough to read — finish this in an afternoon and use it the same night.

The Paycheck-to-Wealth Method

Turning a Regular Salary Into Real Money in Your 20s & 30s

You're not bad with money. You've just been given a broken map.

You went to work. You earned a decent paycheck. You did the responsible-adult things. So why is there nothing left at the end of the month — and why does everyone else seem to have it figured out?

Here's the truth almost no personal-finance book will tell you: it was never about willpower. Decades of behavioral-science research show that the reason smart, hardworking people stay stuck living paycheck to paycheck has almost nothing to do with discipline — and almost everything to do with how our brains are wired and how the modern money world is built. Once you understand that, everything changes.

No shame. No get-rich-quick nonsense. No lectures about your coffee. Just a clear, five-stage system — grounded in real behavioral-economics research — that turns a regular salary into real, growing wealth.

Inside, you'll learn how to:

  • Stop blaming yourself and finally understand why saving has felt impossible
  • Set up a “money machine” that saves and invests automatically in the background of your life
  • Budget in a way you'll actually stick to — the opposite of tracking every dollar
  • Get out of debt using the method the research says you'll actually finish
  • Build the safety net that ends the 3 a.m. money panic
  • Start investing, even if it feels scary or “not for people like me”
  • Grow your income without burning out

You don't need a finance degree. You don't need a big salary. You don't need more discipline. You need a better system — and this is it.

If you're in your 20s or 30s and you're done feeling broke, start here. Future You is counting on it.
