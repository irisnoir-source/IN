Before you put any of this into practice

These books show you how to hand real work — your calendar, your money, your study notes — to an AI. That means handing over information about yourself, and it is worth knowing what happens to it before you start rather than after.

I write about that separately and I would rather you read it first: IrisNoir Thoughts on AI Cybersecurity — what actually happens to the data you type into a chat box, which settings to change in five minutes, and where the current protections genuinely fall short.

No book here asks you to share anything sensitive to get value from it.
